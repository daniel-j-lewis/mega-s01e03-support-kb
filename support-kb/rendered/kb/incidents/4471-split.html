parser: day-first dates read month first

Every tenant on file writes the day first. The shared parser reads month first, so day and month swap whenever the day is 12 or less: 7 April goes out as 4 July. This is the bug #4471 names. Fixed: the parser now reads dd/mm for these locales.

hint: the booking-v3 form asks for MM/DD/YYYY

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People at day-first tenants type what the hint says, and the form reads it the tenant’s way. The date is swapped before any parser sees it, so the parser fix does not help. These reports came in through the form at booking-v3 tenants. Won’t fix: the booking-v3 form is retired, which removes the hint with it. A report like this from now on means a tenant is still on that form.

Reports that read as a one-day move

A few reports describe a date one day off rather than swapped (the desk’s notes list them). They are not a separate problem: their tenants write the day first and they did not come through the booking-v3 form, so their cause is parser and they are counted there, with the footnote under the table.

Why they were merged

Triage merges on the subject line alone (decision 0001). Bugs that all feel like “wrong date” share subject lines, so they merged into one ticket. Read the body and the tenant before merging into a ticket that has a fix queued. Changing the rule needs a new decision record, not an edit to 0001.

Lessons

  • A big ticket can be several bugs that sound the same. Count by cause, not by subject.
  • Check what a fix covers before marking a ticket known.
